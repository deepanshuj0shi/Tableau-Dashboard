# Tableau Dashboard

Interactive Tableau dashboards created using the **Global Superstore** and **Shark Tank US** datasets. The project focuses on exploring sales, profit, customer behavior, investment trends, and business performance through interactive visualizations.

## Dashboard 1: Sales & Profit Performance Analysis

This dashboard provides an overall view of business performance by analyzing sales and profit trends across different regions and states.

### Visualizations

* **Sales and Profit Trend Analysis**

  * Tracks sales and profit over time.
  * Helps identify seasonal patterns and business growth.

* **Profit by State Map**

  * Displays profit distribution across different states.
  * Highlights high-performing and low-performing locations.

* **Sales vs Profit Scatter Plot**

  * Shows the relationship between sales and profit.
  * Helps identify profitable and loss-making transactions.

### Insights

* Sales and profit do not always increase together.
* Some states generate significantly higher profits than others.
* A few transactions have high sales but low profitability.

## Dashboard 2: Regional & Segment Analysis

This dashboard focuses on customer segments, regional performance, and product-level analysis.

### Visualizations

* **Regional Sales Trend**

  * Analyzes sales performance for a selected region over time.
  * Identifies peak and low-performing periods.

* **Segment-wise Sales Comparison**

  * Compares Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
  * Helps understand customer purchasing behavior.

* **Sub-Category Sales Heatmap**

  * Displays sales across product sub-categories and regions.
  * Makes it easy to identify top-performing products.

### Insights

* Consumer segment contributes the highest sales volume.
* Sales performance varies significantly across regions.
* Certain product sub-categories consistently generate higher revenue.

## Dashboard 3: Shark Tank US Investment Analysis

This dashboard analyzes investment trends, startup valuations, industry performance, and investor activity from Shark Tank US data.

### Visualizations

* **Season-wise Valuation Trend**

  * Shows how startup valuations changed across seasons.

* **Ask vs Deal Analysis**

  * Compares entrepreneurs' asking valuations with final deal valuations.

* **Industry Investment Treemap**

  * Displays investment distribution across industries.

* **Investor Leaderboard**

  * Ranks investors based on total investments made.

### Insights

* Startup valuations vary significantly between seasons.
* Some startups secured deals at valuations different from their original ask.
* Investment activity is concentrated in a few major industries.
* Investor participation and total investment differ across Sharks.
